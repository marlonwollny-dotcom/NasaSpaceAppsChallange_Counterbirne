Can you make your mission work?

Real space missions are a web of trade-offs: a better camera needs more power, more power needs bigger solar arrays, bigger arrays add mass, more mass needs a bigger rocket — and the budget is fixed. In Mission Architect you design a complete mission from the science question to the last downlinked bit, fly it day by day, and get a clear explanation of why it succeeded or failed.

Play alone or as a crew of up to four: Mission Lead, Payload Scientist, Systems Engineer and Comms & Data Lead — each with their own goal. You will have to negotiate.

1 · Design

Pick orbit, launcher, instruments, power, comms.

2 · Fly

Launch, cruise, insert, operate. Handle anomalies.

3 · Learn

See the cause-and-effect chain. Improve. Re-fly.
